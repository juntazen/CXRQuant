# Declaration of generative AI use

During the preparation of this work and its revision, the authors used OpenAI Codex (September 2026) and Claude (Anthropic; September–October 2026) to assist with code auditing, development and debugging, CPU/GPU testing, experiment orchestration, statistical replay, figure generation and packaging, and to draft analysis scripts and text describing the analyses; Claude was also used to improve the readability and language of the manuscript. The authors reviewed, revised and verified all code, outputs and text against the stored results and take full responsibility for the content, originality and integrity of this work.

Inputs were supplied by the authors; no result was simulated, and every reported number is regenerated from the archived records by the scripts in this repository.
