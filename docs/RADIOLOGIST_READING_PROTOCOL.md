# Blinded radiologist review protocol (IJIES revision, paper ID 20266214)

> **Status:** this protocol and the analysis tools (`scripts/radiologist_adjudication.py`,
> `scripts/radiologist_analysis.py`) are provided for independent replication. No radiologist review was performed
> for release 2.3.0; finding transitions in this release are FP32-relative extracted-category measures.

## Purpose
Reviewer 2 (comment 3) asks whether the finding transitions measured automatically correspond to clinically
meaningful changes; Reviewer 1 (comment 6) asks for a manually adjudicated extractor-validation subset.

## Readers
- Reader 1 and Reader 2: board-certified radiologists (thoracic/chest imaging experience preferred), reading
  independently.
- Adjudicator: a third, senior radiologist who reads only the items on which Reader 1 and Reader 2 disagree.
- No reader is told which model or numerical precision produced an output, or which output is the full-precision one.

## Material (reader_workbook.xlsx)
- Part A, 150 items: the FINDINGS text given to the model and two impressions (A, B) generated by the same model
  from the same input. The A/B order is random.
  Q1 do A and B differ in any clinical finding; Q2 which; Q3 most severe clinical significance
  (none / minor / significant = could change patient management); Q4 is either impression inconsistent with FINDINGS.
- Part B, 80 impression texts: state of each of the 13 CheXpert categories
  (positive / negative / uncertain / not mentioned).
- Estimated reading time: Part A about 2.5 hours, Part B about 1.5 hours per reader.

## Procedure
1. Each reader receives an identical copy of reader_workbook.xlsx and PROTOCOL.md only.
   The answer key (answer_key_DO_NOT_SHARE.json) stays with the study team.
2. Readers work independently and return `reader_R1.xlsx` / `reader_R2.xlsx`.
3. The study team lists disagreements; the adjudicator receives only those items (same workbook, other rows removed)
   and returns `reader_ADJ.xlsx`.
4. Analysis: `python scripts/radiologist_analysis.py --key answer_key_DO_NOT_SHARE.json --readers reader_R1.xlsx reader_R2.xlsx --adjudicator reader_ADJ.xlsx --out ../results/radiologist_results.json`

## Reported quantities
Inter-reader Cohen's kappa (Q1) and quadratic-weighted kappa (Q3); agreement of the extractor's transition flag
with the consensus (PPV, sensitivity); proportion of clinically significant differences by precision; per-category
kappa between extractor and consensus (Part B).

## Ethics
All texts are de-identified, publicly released IU-XRAY/OpenI reports or model outputs derived from them. The readers
are expert raters, not research subjects; no patient contact or new patient data is involved. Confirm with the
institutional ethics committee whether an exemption letter is needed.

## Acknowledgement / authorship
Readers who only rate are acknowledged by name (with consent); a radiologist who also contributes to design,
interpretation and manuscript revision can qualify for authorship (validation, writing - review and editing).
